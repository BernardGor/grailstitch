Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information GrailStitch (“we”, “us”) at grailstitch.com collects when you visit, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not require accounts and we do not ask you to submit personal information to read the site. If you email us, we keep your message and address to reply to you. Our hosting provider and analytics or advertising partners may automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages viewed.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores your light or dark mode choice in your browser’s local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Advertising and affiliate partners described below may set their own cookies.

Google AdSense and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for consent to personalized advertising through a consent banner before such cookies are used.

eBay Partner Network

Some links on GrailStitch lead to eBay. When you click one, eBay Partner Network and eBay may set cookies to record the referral so that we can earn a commission. See eBay’s User Privacy Notice.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, to object to processing, and, in California, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Email contact@grailstitch.com and we will respond as required by law. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Third-party sites

We link to sites we do not control, including eBay. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
