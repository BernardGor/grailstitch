About GrailStitch

Last updated November 21, 2026

GrailStitch is an independent publication about vintage clothing and footwear from the 1960s to the 2000s. We write for collectors, resellers and anyone who wants to buy a piece with history and know exactly what they are getting.

What we cover

  • Buying guides for leather jackets, denim, knitwear, sneakers and boots.
  • How to date a garment from its tags, stitching, hardware and construction.
  • Condition, sizing and fair-price advice for online marketplaces such as eBay.

How we work

Our articles are researched and written by the GrailStitch editorial team. We compare listings, study manufacturer archives and consult collector communities. When we find an error, we correct it and note the change.

How the site is funded

GrailStitch earns money from advertising and from affiliate links, for example to eBay. This never affects what we recommend. Read the full Affiliate Disclosure.
