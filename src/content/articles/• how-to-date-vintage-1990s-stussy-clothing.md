Long before streetwear became a global luxury industry, Shawn Stüssy began screen-printing his hand-drawn signature onto surfboards and t-shirts in Laguna Beach, California. Throughout the **1990s**, Stüssy expanded from local surf shops to international chapters in New York, Tokyo, and London—producing heavyweight tees, work jackets, and fleece that now command strong collector prices. Because modern Stüssy still uses classic archive graphics, knowing how to read 1990s neck tags, `RN 94974` labels, and single-stitch hems is essential to separating true 1990s pieces from contemporary releases.

## 1. Decode 1990s Stüssy Neck Labels (White Script vs. Black Crown Tag)

Stüssy updated its woven neck labels several times during the 1990s, giving collectors clear timeline markers:

- **1990–1992 (Early White Woven Tag):** Features Shawn Stüssy’s black signature script centered on a crisp white woven label with **`MADE IN U.S.A.`** printed below in simple block letters—without the registration `®` symbol found on later tags.
- **1992–1996 (The Black "Crown" Tag):** One of the most sought-after 1990s Stüssy labels features a black background with white signature lettering and a small **embroidered crown icon** (often in red, gold, or blue) stitched beside or beneath the logo, clearly marked *Made in U.S.A.*
- **1995–1999 (Late-90s White `RN 94974` Tag):** By the mid-to-late 1990s, Stüssy shifted to a wider white woven neck tag displaying the signature script alongside **`RN 94974`** (Stüssy’s official US textile registration number) and *Made in U.S.A.*—a mark that continued until production began moving to Mexico in the early 2000s.

## 2. Single Stitch vs. Double Stitch and Fabric Weight

Inspecting the hems and cotton knit helps confirm whether a 1990s Stüssy tee comes from the first half or second half of the decade:

- **1990–1994 (Single-Stitch Construction):** Early 1990s Stüssy t-shirts were sewn on American tubular blanks featuring **single-stitch sleeve cuffs and bottom hems** (one clean line of thread visible on the outside).
- **1995–1999 (Heavyweight Double-Stitch Blanks):** Around 1995, Stüssy transitioned to heavier, custom-milled 100% cotton blanks finished with **double-needle stitching** on the sleeves and hem and a slightly thicker, tighter crew neck collar.
- **Outerwear & Work Jackets:** Beyond t-shirts, 1990s **Stüssy Outdoor** and workwear pieces (such as canvas chore coats, cargo pants, and fleece pullovers) feature heavy brass **YKK** zippers, custom engraved metal snaps, and secondary woven **Stüssy Outdoor** or **International Stüssy Tribe** patches.

## 3. Spotting Modern Reissues and Counterfeits

Because 1990s Stüssy graphics—especially **World Tour**, **8-Ball**, and **International Tribe** prints—are regularly reissued by the brand today, watch for these three giveaways:

1. **Modern "Made in Mexico" / "Made in Guatemala" Tags:** Contemporary Stüssy tees use a white woven tag explicitly stating *Made in Mexico*, *Guatemala*, or *Honduras* alongside modern multi-language care labels sewn inside the lower left seam.
2. **Oversized Modern Fit vs. Boxy 1990s Cut:** Vintage 1990s Stüssy t-shirts feature a wide, boxy chest with shorter sleeves and torso length; a vintage 1990s **Large** typically measures **21.5 to 22.5 inches pit-to-pit** and **27.5 to 28.5 inches in length**.
3. **Blurry Tag Weaving on Bootlegs:** Counterfeit 1990s Stüssy tags often show fuzzy, uneven edges on the signature script or misspelled care instructions on the reverse side of the neck label.

## Quick 1990s Stüssy Buying Checklist

1. **Verify *Made in U.S.A.* Origin:** Confirm domestic construction on the woven neck label (pre-2000s pieces were predominantly made in the USA).
2. **Check the `RN 94974` Mark:** Look for Stüssy’s registered **`RN 94974`** number on mid-to-late 1990s care tags.
3. **Inspect the Hems:** Single-stitch sleeves point to 1990–1994; heavyweight double-stitch blanks indicate 1995–1999.
4. **Examine Screen-Print Aging:** Look for natural, subtle ink cracking rather than stiff, glossy modern plastisol prints.
5. **Measure Pit-to-Pit:** Confirm flat chest and length measurements to ensure the classic 1990s boxy fit suits your build.
